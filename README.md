# THE WORLD TODAY — Wednesday 18 November 2026

A front-page newspaper image summing up the world news of **Wednesday, 18 November 2026**, built for make.fun's `news-image-20261118` bounty ($250, free entry).

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024) is a hand-built front page, **THE WORLD TODAY**, with the date named in a full-width black banner and again in the footer. Nine stories from 18 November 2026, across regions and fields:

- **Lead — The Gulf:** U.S. strikes Iran's Qeshm Island; five tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz; Iran's Guards hit a U.S.-used base in Bahrain; drones strike U.S. platforms at a Kuwait air base; one crew member missing. With a schematic map of the Strait.
- **Summit — China:** Trump heads to China for the first-ever trilateral with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin; Xi and Putin met in Shenzhen today.
- **Culture — Games:** GTA VI launches tomorrow (19 November); streamers already playing, Kai Cenat's marathon under way.
- **Health — United States:** the U.S. loses its measles-free status as cases spike.
- **Technology — Outages:** NSA email down; T-Mobile, Spectrum and EA hit too — the sixth major incident this month.
- **Politics — United States:** Mary Peltola flips the Alaska Senate seat; Democrats take a 56–44 majority; Hakeem Jeffries nominated to lead the House.
- **Weather — India:** Cyclone Akvan strengthens into a severe cyclonic storm; Odisha moves people to shelters.
- **Also today:** Israel's "concentrated operation" in three areas of central Gaza; the bridge to Crimea crippled by a missile strike; UK inflation at a year high, Nvidia beats forecasts.

The page was written as HTML with real, crisp text (no AI image generation — generated text garbles) and rendered to PNG with headless Chromium. Every headline was checked legible, including at 300 px across.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`). No network, no randomness — two runs write byte-identical output.
